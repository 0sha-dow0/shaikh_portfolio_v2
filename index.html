OnTask NEW

A focus browser that holds you to one task. You set a single task when the browser opens, it is pinned for the whole session, and one relevance judgment is applied across three surfaces: recommendation feeds on any site, cross-domain navigation, and page loads. A bundled MiniLM model decides most items on device; Groq is consulted exactly twice, to expand the task and to break genuine ties. Every hidden item keeps a one-click "show anyway", and the engine fails open on outage but closed on ambiguity, so it never bricks the web.
